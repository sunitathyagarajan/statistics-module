References

Textbooks, measurement standards, the original papers behind each test, and the published sources for the two real cases and the significant figures problems. Each entry notes where it is used in the module.

About the data in this module

The six worked examples, every generated problem and the significant figures problems use realistic numbers made up for teaching. The two real cases use published data, taken from the sources listed under each case below.

Textbooks

  1. Harris, D. C.; Lucy, C. A. Quantitative Chemical Analysis, 10th ed.; W. H. Freeman / Macmillan Learning: New York, 2020.Used for: all key terms, significant figures, confidence intervals, propagation of uncertainty, F test and t test.
  2. Skoog, D. A.; West, D. M.; Holler, F. J.; Crouch, S. R. Fundamentals of Analytical Chemistry, 9th ed.; Brooks/Cole, Cengage Learning: Belmont, CA, 2014.Used for: accuracy and precision, types of error, significant figures, t test.
  3. Miller, J. N.; Miller, J. C. Statistics and Chemometrics for Analytical Chemistry, 7th ed.; Pearson: Harlow, UK, 2018.Used for: significance tests, outliers, calibration, LOD and LOQ.

  2. Taylor, B. N.; Kuyatt, C. E. Guidelines for Evaluating and Expressing the Uncertainty of NIST Measurement Results; NIST Technical Note 1297; National Institute of Standards and Technology: Gaithersburg, MD, 1994. doi:10.6028/NIST.TN.1297Used for: reporting a result with its uncertainty.
  3. NIST/SEMATECH. e-Handbook of Statistical Methods; National Institute of Standards and Technology, 2012. itl.nist.gov/div898/handbookUsed for: normal distribution, t and F distributions, critical values.
  4. Magnusson, B.; Örnemark, U., Eds. Eurachem Guide: The Fitness for Purpose of Analytical Methods — A Laboratory Guide to Method Validation and Related Topics, 2nd ed.; Eurachem, 2014. eurachem.orgUsed for: limit of detection, limit of quantitation, repeatability and reproducibility.
